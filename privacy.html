email-agent privacy policy

email-agent is used only by its owner, on the owner's own Google account. No other person's account is connected to it.

Data it accesses

  • Gmail: the owner's messages and labels, to read, label, archive, trash and restore them, and to place a daily summary in the inbox. It does not send email.
  • Google Calendar: it creates events only after the owner approves each one.
  • Google Drive and Sheets: only the spreadsheet the app itself creates, which holds the owner's settings and a log of changes.

How the data is used and stored

The data is used only to sort the owner's mail and prepare the owner's summary. Results are stored in the owner's own Gmail labels and Google Sheet. To classify a message, the app sends its sender, subject and text to Anthropic's Claude API, which returns category labels. Nothing is sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone else.

email-agent's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

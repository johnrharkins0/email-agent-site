email-agent

email-agent is a personal assistant used by one person, its owner, on their own Google account. It is not offered to anyone else.

Each morning it sorts the owner's Gmail by applying labels, archiving, and moving approved promotional senders to Trash. It lists dated items the owner can approve for Google Calendar, and places a short summary in the owner's inbox. It never sends email.
